🚀 DEPLOYMENT STEPS (QUICK VERSION)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to https://replit.com → "+ Create" → "Import from GitHub"
2. Paste: https://github.com/donyp/arsipankagdrive
3. Klik Import (tunggu ~2 menit)

   - SUPABASE_URL = [dari Supabase dashboard]
   - SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY = [dari Supabase]

5. Klik "Run" button
6. Tunggu sampai muncul: ✅ Backend listening on port 5000

7. Aplikasi live di: https://[replit-username].repl.co
